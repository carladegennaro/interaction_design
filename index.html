Eyes on
Earth

Developed on the occasion of NASA's 70th anniversary, this project examines the ongoing impact of climate change through the study of five key Earth-observing satellites. The initiative is designed to illustrate how these spacecraft operate and why their data is so important for scientific research and for monitoring our planet’s health.
