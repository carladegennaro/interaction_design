Eyes on
Earth

On the occasion of NASA's seventieth anniversary, founded in 1958, this project explores the agency's digital archives to highlight the importance of aerospace technology in monitoring our planet's health. By navigating through five key satellites, you will discover how the conquest of space has become the fundamental tool for understanding global changes. Start your journey to see Earth as you have never seen it before.
